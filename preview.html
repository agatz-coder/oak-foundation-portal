Who We Are

About OAK Foundation

Our Mission

OAK Foundation commits its resources to address issues of global, social, and environmental concern, particularly those that have a major impact on the lives of the disadvantaged.

Founded in 1983 by Jette and Alan Parker, the Foundation makes grants to organisations around the world that share a commitment to addressing the root causes of the problems and contributing to lasting, systemic change.

How We Work

We work through six programme areas, each led by experienced staff who partner closely with grantees. Our approach values trust-based philanthropy, long-term relationships, and systemic change.

Safeguarding

OAK Foundation is committed to safeguarding the welfare and safety of children and adults at risk. We expect all partners and grantees to share this commitment.
